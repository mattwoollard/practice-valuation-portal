Welcome to the Sonic Clinical Services valuation portal

Thank you for considering a partnership with Sonic Clinical Services. This portal collects the information we need to value your practice. Your answers save as you go, so you can leave at any time and come back to finish later.

1. Tell us about the practiceLocations, premises, patients, workflow and fees. Most questions take a short answer.
2. Add your doctors and staffOne row per doctor and per employee. This replaces the Doctor Information and Employee Information forms.
3. Upload your documentsP&Ls, billing reports, fixed asset register and lease. Then review and submit.
Before you start, it helps to have: your P&L for the valuation period and the last 3 financial years, a billing report showing gross billings by doctor, your fixed asset register, a wages breakdown, and your lease.
Everything you provide is treated as confidential under the Mutual Confidentiality Agreement you signed in step one and is only shared with the Sonic team preparing your valuation. Please do not upload any patient names or clinical records.
